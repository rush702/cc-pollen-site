Before you order

What is bee pollen, really?

It is flower pollen that honeybees pack into granules. One spoon can carry many plant species. CC Pollen blends regions on purpose so the jar is not a single-color, single-crop pollen.

How do I start the granules?

Day one: two or three granules. Double each day until you reach about one teaspoon, roughly 3 grams. Eat them straight, or stir into yogurt, oatmeal, or a smoothie. Keep the jar cold.

Why the fuss about refrigeration?

Most pollen Royden found in the 1970s was imported and heat-treated so it would not mold. The company has said that kind of handling can destroy a large share of the food value. High Desert pollen is cold from collection through shipment.

Who should not take hive products?

Skip them if you are allergic to bee products, pollen, or bee stings. Honey is not for infants under 12 months. If you are pregnant, nursing, on medication, or under a doctor’s care, ask that clinician first.
